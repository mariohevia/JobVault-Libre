Support This Project

Help keep JobVault Libre free, open-source, and actively maintained.

JobVault Libre is a free, open-source app built to make tracking job applications easier. Everything runs locally on your computer: no cloud services, no tracking, no funny business.

It's developed in spare time, and it'll always be free: no paywalls, no subscriptions. Your data never leaves your machine.

If you're finding it useful and want to help keep it going, any support is genuinely appreciated. It helps carve out the time to maintain and improve the app for everyone.
